Everything you need to record

Nothing you don't. Just the tools you use every day.

Screen video

Record the whole screen, one window, or a region. Up to 1080p and 60 FPS, saved as MKV.

Screenshots

The whole screen, one window, or any region. Saved as PNG with one press.

GIF and WebP

Capture a short animation for a chat or an article. ezcap builds the GIF or WebP.

Clean audio

System sound and the microphone together, with a separate mic boost. Audio only is fine too.

Region editor

Select an area, then draw an arrow, add text, or cover something. Save it, copy it, or record it.

Always at hand

It lives in the tray. Click the icon and recording starts. Recent files can be copied or shown in the folder.

Looks simple. Works simply.
